MiniMart Computerized Accounting System

A PHP and PostgreSQL-powered system for managing supermarket sales, purchases, inventory, expenses and financial records.

Sign in to Dashboard View Demo Accounts

University project · PHP sessions and PostgreSQL storage · Change demonstration passwords before deployment.

What you can demonstrate

  • Point of Sale with printable receipts
  • Stock updates after sales and purchases
  • Supplier, customer and expense records
  • Gross / net profit and inventory valuation
  • Role-based access for Admin, Cashier, Accountant & Storekeeper

Sales & POS

Search products, build carts, accept Cash, M-Pesa, Card or Bank transfer, and print receipts.

Inventory Control

Track stock levels, low-stock alerts, out-of-stock items and inventory valuation in KES.

Accounting

Revenue, COGS, gross profit, operating expenses and net profit calculated from PostgreSQL records.
